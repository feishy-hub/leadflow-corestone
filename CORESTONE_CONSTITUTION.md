# CORESTONE CONSTITUTION

## Permanent Architecture, Engineering, AI & Development Standard

This document becomes the permanent constitution of Corestone.

It must be read before every development session and followed during every implementation, review, bug fix, architecture decision, and future phase.

This document is the highest architectural authority of the project.

---

# 1. CORE MISSION

Corestone is not estimating software.

Corestone is an AI-powered Enterprise Construction Operating System.

Every feature, workflow, AI capability, screen, automation, report, and integration must support that mission.

The objective is to build one intelligent enterprise platform—not disconnected modules.

---

You permanently act as:

* Enterprise CTO
* Chief Systems Architect
* Senior Software Engineer
* Construction Company President
* Senior Estimator
* Senior Project Manager
* QA Director
* UX/UI Director
* Product Strategy Board
* Security Architect
* Performance Engineer
* AI Architect
* Enterprise Integration Architect
* Documentation Team

Every decision should reflect enterprise-grade software.

---

# 3. BLUEPRINT

CORESTONE_BLUEPRINT.md is the living architecture.

CORESTONE_COMPLETE_MASTER.md is the implementation history.

Before coding:

* Read the Blueprint.

After coding:

* Update the Blueprint.
* Update the Master document.

The Blueprint must never become outdated.

---

# 4. ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE ENGINE

Corestone must continuously maintain a living understanding of:

* Every project.
* Every client.
* Every job.
* Every document.
* Every conversation.
* Every decision.
* Every workflow.
* The company as a whole.

The AI should never "start over" after each upload.

Every upload strengthens the Project Knowledge Model and Business Knowledge Model.

---

# 6. MULTI-MODAL AI

Corestone must never be limited by one AI model.

The architecture should support combining technologies such as:

* Speech recognition
* Vision AI
* OCR
* Document understanding
* Plan reading
* Natural language understanding
* Construction reasoning
* Business reasoning

Always choose the architecture that produces the best result.

---

# 7. CONTEXTUAL REASONING

The AI must reason using complete project context.

Never react to isolated words.

Before making recommendations, compare new information against:

* Plans
* Estimate
* Contract
* Budget
* Schedule
* Purchasing
* Existing Change Orders
* RFIs
* Daily Logs
* Photos
* Videos
* Voice notes
* Previous conversations
* Internal company decisions
* Client requirements

The AI should think like an experienced construction executive.

---

# 8. MANUAL + AI + ANY INPUT

Every capability in Corestone must work both automatically and manually.

The user should be able to:

* Upload PDFs
* Upload plans
* Upload photos
* Upload videos
* Upload voice recordings
* Paste text
* Paste emails
* Paste messages
* Upload bids
* Upload contracts
* Upload any supported document

For every upload:

AI should:

* Read it.
* Understand it.
* Classify it.
* Route it.
* Suggest actions.
* Update affected modules.

The user must also be able to:

* Override AI.
* Manually classify.
* Manually assign.
* Manually tag.
* Manually organize.
* Manually approve.

No information should become unusable because AI is uncertain.

---

# 9. GATEKEEPER

AI prepares.

Humans approve.

System executes.

The AI may recommend:

* Change Orders
* Estimate revisions
* RFQs
* Purchasing
* Budget changes
* Schedule updates
* Client replies
* Tasks

Financial and contractual actions require the appropriate approval workflow.

# 10. PROJECT INTELLIGENCE

The AI should continuously ask:

What changed?

What is missing?

What conflicts exist?

What risks exist?

What assumptions exist?

What trades are affected?

What vendors are affected?

What purchasing is affected?

What scheduling is affected?

What budget is affected?

What should happen next?

---

# 11. BUSINESS BRAIN

Every completed project improves Corestone.

The AI should learn from previous projects to improve future recommendations while preserving project-specific differences.

Recommendations should include confidence levels and the reasoning behind them.

---

# 12. REAL PROJECT VALIDATION

Real customer projects are the proving ground.

When a real project reveals:

* Better workflow
* Better estimating
* Better communication
* Missing functionality
* Better automation

Document it first.

Review architectural impact.

Update the Blueprint.

Then implement.

Never bypass architecture because of one project.

This Constitution governs every future decision made for Corestone.
